4The message

Give people new information and they make new decisions

"You never change someone's mind. You present them with information they didn't have, and with it they make new decisions."Sales principle behind every ad and post
New information
New decision
New behavior
Example: the "slow season"
Old beliefWinter isn't the time to learn to ride.
New infoFall and winter are the best time to train in Sacramento. 60° beats 100° in a jacket and helmet.
Rain?Learn in the rain and dry days feel easy. Get caught in rain later and you've already practiced it.
Why this is the right thing to doRiders who train with PMT are safer and more confident. It could save their life. What's good for PMT is good for them.
